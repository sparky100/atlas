### 11. Customer & Metering

- **11.3 Guaranteed Standards Payments** — Paying customers when guaranteed standards of performance are missed.
- **11.4 Connection Quotations** — Providing quotations and offers for new and modified connections.

### 12. Regulatory Compliance & Price-Control Reporting

- **12.5 Industry Codes Compliance** — Meeting obligations under the distribution and grid codes.
